Competitive landscape

Competition

Direct

PatientACE

Tests I/E criteria before study launch by running live recruitment campaigns across candidate site catchments, then ranks the sites.

Same question, opposite method. Ad campaigns and web pre-screeners do not reach exon-51-amenable boys, and cannot capture CTG repeat length or exon deletion status.

Direct

Phesi

Trial Accelerator. Per-criterion impact on the candidate pool, pre-lock, with alternative criteria that broaden eligibility.

Built on licensed patient data across all indications rather than published natural history in one.

Direct

Medidata

Protocol Optimization. Simulates design changes against enrollment before study start-up.

Enterprise scale, broad indication coverage.

Benchmark

C-Path D-RSC

DMD trial simulator. Models NSAA, FVC and timed function tests. EMA Letter of Support. Free.

Requires R and pharmacometrics expertise, gated behind a data use committee. Reference standard to validate against.

Precedent

Unlearn.AI

Digital twin control arms. CHMP Qualification Opinion for PROCOVA. GxP, 21 CFR Part 11, SOC 2.

A regulator accepted model output as part of a trial's evidence.

Adjacent

TriNetX · ConcertAI · Clinerion

Licensed EHR and claims data, federated queries across hospital networks.

Molecular eligibility is not in claims data and inconsistent in EHR.

Incumbent

Large CROs

Feasibility bundled into a full-service trial contract.

Revenue is denominated in the time this removes.
